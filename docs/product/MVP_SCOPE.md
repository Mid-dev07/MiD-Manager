# MVP Scope

## Identity
- Email/password authentication
- Google OAuth
- MFA foundation
- Private profile with name, email, avatar
- Personalization and limited guest mode
- Export/delete account foundation

## Daily Management
- Daily Schedule with recurring activities
- Day/Week/Agenda views
- Reminders and conflict detection
- Tasks, subtasks, deadlines, status
- Priority Engine with explainable prioritization

## Finance
- Manual financial accounts and transactions
- Categories
- Budget and savings goals
- Basic analytics
- Receipt capture/extraction with user confirmation
- External bank/e-wallet sync is optional and not an MVP dependency

## Goals & Habits
- Measurable monthly/custom goals
- Goal progress and milestones
- Habit frequency/tracking/consistency
- Basic personal achievements

## Health & Activity
- Basic activity recording/history
- Goal/habit linkage

## AI
- Natural-language task/schedule entry
- Daily briefing
- Priority explanations
- Receipt intelligence
- Basic finance/goal/task queries

## Integrations
- Google Workspace foundation
- Telegram foundation
- WhatsApp remains optional and cost/provider dependent
