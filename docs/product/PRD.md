# MiD Product Requirements Document

## Vision
MiD is a personal self-management system that connects time, tasks, money, health, habits, and goals into one context-aware experience.

## Core Problem
Users often manage schedules, tasks, finance, health, habits, and goals across disconnected tools. The product should reduce fragmentation, cognitive load, and uncertainty about what matters next.

## Product Value
- Awareness: understand what is happening.
- Prioritization: understand what matters now.
- Action: know what to do next.

## Product Loop
Plan -> Schedule -> Act -> Track -> Reflect -> Improve.

## Target Evolution
Personal use -> small-scale multi-user -> public/global product.

## Core Modules
Identity & Profile; Daily Schedule; Task Management; Priority Engine; Finance; Health & Activity; Goals; Habits; Achievement; Notifications; Google Workspace; Telegram; WhatsApp; AI Intelligence.

## Product Principles
User control; privacy first; AI as intelligence layer; core independence from AI/external services; modularity; progressive complexity; low cognitive load; free-tier-first.

## Non-Goals for MVP
Full bank/e-wallet transaction capabilities; complex social analytics; autonomous general agent; microservices; large RAG/vector infrastructure; large-scale multi-user architecture.
